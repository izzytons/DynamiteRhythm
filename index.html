Meet our newest band member!

Jeff Meyer

Jeff has been playing bass guitar since his teenage years, quickly developing a deep love for the instrument. Over the years, he has performed in numerous musical theater productions throughout southern New England, as well as with various local bands.
When he's not on stage, Jeff enjoys spending quality time with close friends and family, as well as getting outdoors for camping and cheering on the Patriots. He lives in Putnam, CT with his wife and their two amazing boys. A 2008 graduate of Johnson & Wales University, he holds degrees in Marketing and Management. Professionally, he has built a career in the insurance industry—but music has always remained his true passion.

Dynamite Rhythm has been providing Southern New England with an authentic Rock & Roll Dance Party since 2016. This tight-knit quintet strives to bring new life to the the classic sounds of your favorite 45's!


Loosely named from the Northern Rhode Island exclusive "Dynamite" sandwich, this group entertains audiences of all ages!


Carefully selecting songs spanning 50 years of jukebox gold, Dynamite Rhythm's 4 vocalists all sing lead and harmony on top of a solid rhythm section. Add a sizzling trumpet on top and you've got one "Dynamite" show!
